C · Quiet Ink

Night-first and editorial. Premium by subtraction, not decoration.

Type
Newsreader italic for the wordmark and lane names, Hanken Grotesk for everything else.
Colour
Near-black ground; colour reduced to a single edge marking which lane a note came from.
The risk
Least sticky-note of the three. It may read as a developer tool, not a calm board.

What I'd pick, and why

B, pulled back about 30%. Your audience is students on Android phones, and the feeling you named is relief, not focus. Warmth earns trust faster than restraint does, and the handwriting on note text makes the board feel like yours rather than the app's.

A is the safer, more obviously "premium" answer and would age better. C is the most distinctive and the furthest from the brief.

What would help most:

  • Which of the three is closest — even if none is right.
  • What the first design got wrong. "Too corporate", "too childish", "too busy" is enough to steer the next pass.
  • Whether still. survives as the name, since the wordmark is doing real work in all three.
