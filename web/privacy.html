Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026. This is the whole policy — there is no longer version.

The short version. still. has no server. Signed out, nothing you write ever leaves your phone, because there is nowhere for it to go. Signed in, one file goes to a private folder in your own Google Drive that only this app can open. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third party we hand anything to.

What is stored on your phone

Your notes, which section each is in, when you wrote it, any urgency or reminder you set, and two settings: the theme you picked and when the board last synced. All of it sits in a database inside the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes it.

If you never sign in

Then that is the entire story. The app makes no network requests of its own. It does not know who you are, and neither do we.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and exists for one reason: so your board is on every phone you sign in on.

  • What Google tells the app: your email address, your name, and the fact that you granted access. Held in memory while the app is open and not written to disk. Your email is shown on the profile screen so you can see which account you are on.
  • What the app puts in your Drive: one JSON file containing your notes, inside appDataFolder — a hidden space Drive keeps for each app. It does not appear in your Drive, other apps cannot read it, and still. cannot read anything else in your Drive. Not one other file. That is enforced by Google, not by us: the app only ever asks for the drive.appdata permission.
  • Who else sees it: nobody. The file is in your account. It is never sent to us, because there is no us to send it to — no server, no database, no logs.

Signing out forgets the token immediately. Your notes stay on the phone, and the file stays in your Drive until you delete it.

Deleting what is there

  • On the phone: delete a note, or uninstall the app.
  • In your Drive: open Drive settings → Manage apps → still. → Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the app's access entirely at your Google account permissions.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone. Nothing is sent through a server, and the app does not use push notifications at all.

Children

still. is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing that would identify anyone, signed in or out.

Changes

If this ever changes — particularly if anything new leaves the phone — this page changes with it and the date above moves. The app is open source, so the claims here can be checked against the code rather than taken on trust.
