## C1 — progress

Three identities drafted and applied to the same lane of notes:
[directions.html](../directions.html).

| | Direction | Type | Stance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Studio | Archivo, one family | Precise, unhurried. The Notion end of the brief. |
| **B** | Playground | Gabarito + Shantell Sans on note text | Warm, tactile, slightly askew. The Duolingo end. |
| **C** | Quiet Ink | Newsreader italic + Hanken Grotesk | Night-first, editorial, colour reduced to one edge. |

**Decided: B, dialled back roughly 30%** — see [design tokens](07-design-tokens.md).

The reasoning — the audience is students on
Android phones and the promised feeling is relief, not focus. A is the safer
premium answer; C is the most distinctive but least sticky-note.

**Not blocking any more**, but still useful: what the rejected first design got
wrong. It would tell us whether the pull-back went far enough or too far.

**Related:** Q1 (final name) is now the live question — the wordmark is set type
in Gabarito, and the name is the part still unsettled.
